# Universal Product Marketing Context & Multi-Business Guidelines

This document provides product marketing grounding for the multi-agent Google Ads team across various business verticals. Consult this context before drafting ad creative, selecting negative keywords, or tuning bidding strategies.

---

| Business Type | Key Objective | Primary Target Metric | Secondary Signals | Typical Ad Formats |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`ecommerce`** | Online Purchases & Revenue | **Target ROAS** (3.0x - 5.0x+) | Cart Additions, AOV, Product CVR | Performance Max, Google Shopping, Search RSAs |

## 2. Business-Specific Negative Keyword Rules

- **E-Commerce**: Disqualify non-commercial searches (`how to make`, `diy`, `broken`, `manual`, `wholesale`, `jobs`, `salary`), but preserve high-intent commercial terms (`buy`, `order`, `store`, `cheap`, `discount`, `coupon`, `fast shipping`).
- **B2B SaaS**: Disqualify job seekers, students, and open-source searchers (`jobs`, `salary`, `internship`, `github`, `source code`, `course`, `crack`, `free download`).
- **Local Services**: Disqualify DIY tutorials and distant regions (`how to fix yourself`, `youtube`, `license exam`, `school`, non-serviced cities/states).
- **Lead Gen**: Disqualify current customers seeking support or government assistance (`login`, `portal`, `customer service number`, `grants`, `free government`, `complaints`).
- **Mobile Apps**: Disqualify desktop platforms and piracy (`pc`, `windows`, `mac`, `emulator`, `apk mod`, `cheat codes`).

## 3. Ad Copywriting Rules (Grounded in `ad-creative` & `copywriting`)

- **Strict Length Limits**:
  - Headlines: Exactly $\le 30$ characters (including spaces).
  - Descriptions: Exactly $\le 90$ characters (including spaces).
- **Zero AI Clichés**:
  - Never use: *"In today's fast-paced world"*, *"Look no further"*, *"Say goodbye to X"*, *"Unlock your potential"*, *"Game changer"*, or *"It's not X, it's Y"*.
- **Proven Angles by Model**:
  - *E-Commerce*: Price anchors ("From $49"), shipping incentives ("Free 2-Day Delivery"), review counts ("Rated 4.9★ by 20,000+"), risk reversal ("30-Day Free Returns").
  - *B2B SaaS*: Time-to-value ("Deploy in Under 10 Mins"), compliance ("SOC-2 Type II Certified"), measurable outcome ("Save 15 Hours Weekly").
  - *Local Services*: Speed & location ("Near You Today", "24/7 Rapid Response"), trust ("Licensed & Insured", "15+ Years Local Experience").
  - *Lead Gen*: Low friction ("Compare Rates in 2 Mins", "No Obligation Quote"), simplicity ("1-Page Fast Form").
